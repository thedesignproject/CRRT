Pixel-art with intent. The carrot from a CRT.

A working brand system for CRRT.AI — a visual feedback widget for designers. The pixel-art carrot is not nostalgia for its own sake; it's a semantic match for the name. CRRT and CRT (cathode-ray tube) share an alphabet. The carrot rendered in pixels is what the carrot looked like on the screens that invented the pixel.

CRRT ≈ CRT. The name and the visual share a world. That's the hook.
/ 01

The canonical mark

One asset, one canonical lockup. The pixel-art carrot rendered on a black circular ground. Diagonal orientation, multi-shade orange body, two-tone green leaves. This is the brand.

/ 02

Three rules

The brand operates on three constraints. Each one keeps the system from drifting into "just retro" or "just toy."

01.

Pixel-art is contained

The pixel grid lives only in the carrot icon. The wordmark stays in clean Inter sans. Mixing pixel-everything turns the brand into a video game. Mixing pixel-icon + sans-wordmark keeps it tech.

02.

Monochrome by default

Black, white, cream. Carrot orange and leaf green appear in the logo; outside the logo, the system is monochrome with carrot orange as accent. No fluorescent neon.

03.

The .AI is a TLD, not a slogan

Treated as a domain suffix, not a positioning claim. Smaller weight, sometimes lighter color. The brand is CRRT; the .AI is where you find it.

The widget

The product itself. A floating circular trigger that anchors to any page. Hover (or focus) reveals the two primary actions — drop a new carrot, or open the existing list. Try it: hover the carrot in the bottom-right of the demo browser below.

/ State 01

Default — closed

56×56 canonical logo, anchored bottom-right with 24px padding. The black circle is the trigger; the pixel-art carrot is the face. Pulsing carrot-orange dot in top-right indicates unresolved feedback count. Resting state should feel like a tab on the page, not a pop-up.

/ State 02

Hover / focus — expanded

Two pill options fly upward with 8px stagger and opacity fade. "Drop a carrot" is the primary action — uses the canonical mark as its leading icon. "See feedback" shows live count of unresolved items in VT323. Click outside to dismiss.
