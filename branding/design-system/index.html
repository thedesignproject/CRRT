Six logos.
One carrot.

A working set of mark proposals for Carrot, a visual feedback widget. Each logo is built around the same insight — the carrot shape doubles as a map pin — but executes that idea at different points on the spectrum from organic to geometric. Color is held tight: ink, cream, and a single carrot-orange accent. No green leaves; the leaf-shape becomes a downward arrow that says "drop here."

§ 01

Logo proposals

Six directions, listed loosest to tightest. Each carrot uses the single-orange accent. The "leaf" element is treated as a downward arrow — the gesture of dropping a pin — instead of organic foliage.

№ 01 · Arrow-Leaf

Single-stem arrow

One bold downward arrow as the stalk. Geometric teardrop body. Closest to the reference you shared, but reduced to a single mark instead of three. Reads first as a pin, second as a carrot.

Recommended App-icon ready Mono
№ 02 · Trident

Three-leaf carrot

Center stalk with two curved side leaves. The most overtly "vegetable" of the proposals — preserves the silhouette of a real carrot top. Slightly more organic feel.

Organic Reference-faithful
№ 03 · Negative Space

Cut-out app icon

Solid orange rounded square with the carrot shape carved out of the cream. App-icon native — looks finished in iOS/Mac dock at any size. Most modern/tech execution.

App-icon native Most tech
№ 04 · Outline

Geometric line

Pure outline, no fills except the orange stroke. Stripe / Vercel territory — minimal and architectural. Loses some warmth but gains tech-modern legibility at small sizes.

Minimal Stripe-style
№ 05 · Pin-Hybrid

Maps-pin lean

Bigger circular "drop point" indicator pushes this past "carrot" into "Maps pin." Most explicitly product-coded — a user sees a pin first. Loses some of the brand personality.

Pin-first Product-coded
№ 06 · Reduced

Single-shape carrot

Stem and body merged into one continuous shape. Most reduced execution — works at favicon sizes and as an emoji-replacement. Closest to the kind of mark Slack or Mailchimp would use.

Smallest-size friendly Iconic
