/ 01

The name

CRRT is carrot with the vowels removed. The contraction happens to share an alphabet with CRT — the cathode-ray tube. The pixel-art carrot is what follows: a carrot rendered as it would have looked on the screens that invented the pixel.

/ 01
The vegetable
CARROT

The literal subject. A "carrot" is also the unit of input — users drop a carrot on what they want to flag.

/ 02
Drop the vowels
CARROT → CRRT

No vowels. The mark contracts; the pronunciation stays "carrot." The visual identity gets compact enough to live on a 16-pixel grid.

/ 03
It's also a tube
CRRT ≈ CRT

Same alphabet as cathode-ray tube. The pixel-art carrot is the visual hook — a sprite from the era of CRT screens.

CRRT ≈ CRT. The name and the visual share a world. That's the hook.

Bonus: .AI is treated as a TLD, not a slogan. Smaller weight, often lighter color. The brand is CRRT; the .AI is where you find it.

/ 02

Operational wordmark

A second canonical lockup. CRRT.AI says where we live; CRRT.>_ says what we do. Every glyph is real shell syntax: the dot is method access, > is stream redirect, _ is the terminal cursor — always blinking, always ready.
