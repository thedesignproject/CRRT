How it works

Three steps. No meetings.

No. 01

Plant a script

Drop one line of JavaScript in your <head>. Carrot springs up wherever it's needed. No SDKs, no rebuilds.

No. 02

Drop a carrot

Click anywhere on any page. A carrot appears. Type your note. Tag a teammate. The pin sticks to the element, not the pixel.

No. 03

Watch it grow

Comments turn into tickets. Tickets turn into commits. Designs improve. Bugs wilt. Your team ships fewer arguments.

What's in the basket

Small features. Quietly considered.

Element-anchored pins

Pins stick to elements, not coordinates. Resize, restyle, or redesign — the carrot follows.

Threaded notes

Each carrot is a thread. Reply, tag, mention. Resolve when the field's been weeded.

Linear, GitHub, Figma

Pins flow into your tools. A carrot dropped here becomes a ticket there. No double entry.

Works on any framework

React, Vue, plain HTML, Webflow, Framer, WordPress. If it renders, Carrot grows on it.

Read-only mode for clients

Share a public link. Stakeholders drop carrots without seeing internal threads or resolved notes.

Self-host or cloud

Run Carrot on our infrastructure or yours. Open core, with the paid bits clearly labeled.
