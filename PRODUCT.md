# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Designers, founders, product managers, and independent builders who notice UI problems and use coding agents to implement fixes. They need to preserve visual context, specify the intended outcome, and review the result. See `docs/context.md` and `rules/business.md` for product positioning.

## Product Purpose

CRRT connects visual feedback on a working web product to scoped instructions for a coding agent. Success means reviewed fixes shipped, with a shorter path from noticing an issue to verifying its resolution.

## Brand Personality

Confident, premium, calm. Speak to competent peers with specific outcomes and active language. Humans approve the feedback and review the agent's changes.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI feature marketing, gamification, decorative gradients, nested cards, and sprawling project-management interfaces. The resource library should teach practical work connected to CRRT's feedback-to-fix workflow. See the explicit boundaries in `rules/business.md`.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the existing CRRT site and canonical tokens rather than introduce a separate visual identity.
- Lead with the outcome a reader can achieve in one bounded task.
- Make source material, reusable examples, and review criteria easy to find.
- Keep human judgment visible throughout agent workflows.
- Offer useful content before asking a visitor to sign up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the existing keyboard, focus, contrast, responsive layout, and reduced-motion requirements in `rules/ux.md` and `branding/CRRT-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`. Keep long-form content readable and all essential navigation available without animation.
